Business recommendations

  1. Invest more in series. TV Shows rose from 25% of additions in 2018 to 34% in 2021, yet 67% of shows have only one season. Renewing proven shows would build lasting audiences.
  2. Grow regional content. India (1,057), the UK (638) and Pakistan (421) follow the US (3,240), and International Movies is the top category. Pakistan is TV-led (350 of 421 titles), a clear niche to expand.
  3. Focus on quality over volume. Titles per release year peaked in 2018 (1,146) and the model forecasts about 970-980 per year, so growth will come from better selection, not more titles.
  4. Look at family content. Only 10% of titles are rated for kids, while 46% target adults. Check this gap against viewing data before investing.
  5. Use "more like this" recommendations. The content-based recommender matched genres about 9 times better than random picks, which can support discovery.
  6. Fix metadata gaps. 29% of titles have no director listed. Complete data improves search, recommendations and models.
The dataset has no viewership or revenue data, so these are analysis-based suggestions to validate with business data.
